OBSERVED FLOWERING · 2 SEPTEMBER 2026

A little pink before the leaves.

Look along the dark branches: some buds are still tightly closed, while others have opened into soft layers of pink. With so few leaves in this photograph, the flowers are easy to pick out.

Likely an ornamental flowering plum
Possibly Prunus × blireana · Identification pending.

Its double pink flowers fit this candidate, but related flowering peaches and apricots can look similar. A closer look at the leaves will help narrow it down.

What might change on your next visit?

This stop records one spring afternoon, not a live flowering report. Look for fallen petals, new leaves and buds opening. More photographed stops will turn this first visit into a neighbourhood loop.

Identification & field notes
