What it does

Background

Apple aerials

Streams the same Apple TV aerial footage as Aerial Dream, muted. The next clip buffers ahead for smooth changes. Playback stops the moment another app is in front.

Home

Your apps, nothing else

Large favourites with their own TV banners, then everything else in one row. Hold OK to reorder, hide or add favourites.

Setup

No computer needed

A first-run wizard makes the remote’s Home button open Drift through an optional accessibility helper, with no adb required.

Music

Now playing

Track, artist, album and cover from Qobuz or any player. Long names scroll gently.

Night

Dim mode

While an album plays, the screen fades to near-black with only a faint track and clock. The aerial stops, so the box isn’t decoding video while you listen.

OLED

Burn-in care

The clock and text drift a little each minute, further once the screen has faded, and static text is dimmed. Drift keeps the screen awake at home, so your normal screensaver keeps working in other apps.

Install

  1. Get the APK onto the TV. Use an app like Downloader with this address: andrewf13.github.io/Andrew-s-Playground/downloads/drift-2.0.apk. Or from a computer: adb install -r drift-2.0.apk
  2. Open Drift. The Home screen wizard appears. Choose Turn on Home button helper, switch on “Drift Home button” in Accessibility, then press Back.
  3. Optional: now playing. In Drift’s Settings, set Now playing to Any player. On Google TV, allow notification access when asked. On the Shield, the Home button helper covers it.
  4. Optional: night listening. Settings → Fade to → Dim when music plays.

Good to know

Aerials: Drift plays Apple’s publicly hosted aerial videos over plain http, the way Aerial Dream does, because Android doesn’t trust that server’s certificate. You can also copy your own videos into Android/data/com.drift.launcher/files/Aerials to play them offline.

Accessibility helper: it acts only when the built-in home screen appears, and it keeps only the player link from music notifications. It never reads what’s on screen and ignores other notifications.

Diagnostics: the bottom of Drift’s Settings shows where the aerials came from and any recent crash.
